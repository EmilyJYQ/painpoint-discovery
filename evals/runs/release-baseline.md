## Concrete next step

Ask the requester to supply, with permission, the retrieval workflow and an anonymized example file containing a known highlighted quote, and access to the reporter or another relevant student with a comparable real failure. This is a proposed request, not a completed interview or recruitment commitment.

Run a bounded comparison of the user's usual retrieval method, the documented annotation search, and a short manual/workflow explanation. Verify that the quote truly exists before timing. Record success, time in seconds, setup time in minutes, unsupported file cases and any lost or altered annotations. Baseline values remain unmeasured.

Proposed experiment cap: one 45-minute session with one willing participant, no paid purchase, and no new software build. Proposed decision rules, not established benchmarks:

- If documented search finds the known quote within two minutes after at most ten minutes of setup and the participant considers that acceptable, stop new-build exploration for this case; offer workflow guidance. This does not settle the wider market.
- If setup or an unsupported file format explains failure, revise the workflow or inspect a compatible existing option before coding.
- If the quote remains unretrievable after supported setup and the participant confirms recurring, material consequences, gather a second real task and assess a narrowly bounded retrieval prototype with explicit data access and success criteria.
- If permission, materials or participant access cannot be obtained, retain “gather evidence”; do not substitute simulated interviews.

Keep P01 separate. To elevate it, obtain a target student's real input notes and desired table, establish the task cost, then compare the free plugin against a manual baseline under that student's constraints. No installation or trial is implied here.

No development handoff is justified yet. The next decision depends on real workflow reproduction and the fit of existing alternatives, not on combining the two complaints into an assumed literature-matrix product.
