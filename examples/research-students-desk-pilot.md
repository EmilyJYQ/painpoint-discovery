# Real desk-research pilot: research students

[中文说明](../README.md) · [English overview](../README.en.md)

Conducted on 2026-09-27 using public web search and source pages. This is a condensed, author-run pilot, not an interview study, a controlled benchmark or a product endorsement. No software was installed or tested. Source content may change after the observation date.

## What the search found

The exploration compared literature-note reuse, research-file organization, and supervisor review across writing tools. It narrowed to turning existing reading notes into a literature comparison table. The other two directions were explored less deeply, so this is a shortlist decision, not a market ranking.

## The counterevidence changed the decision

[Zotero documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/pdf_reader) describes notes from annotations with links back to source pages. Better Notes has an [Annotation Matrix template](https://github.com/windingwind/zotero-better-notes/discussions/1033), first posted in 2024 with the then-tested versions. The [Literature Review with LLM project](https://github.com/kaguratart/literature-review-with-llm) also describes evidence-labeled matrix output. These are documented alternatives; current compatibility and ease of use were not tested.

**Decision: gather evidence and test reuse first.** Public questions support task friction, but not a claim that matrix-generation software is missing. A possible residual opportunity is reducing setup and transferring existing notes into the desired structure. That remains a hypothesis.

## A falsifiable next step

Propose three research students, each with notes from five already-read papers. Observe their current task, then test an appropriate existing solution on comparable materials. Record setup, review and correction time as well as content/source preservation. Participants, files and outcomes have not been obtained.

If existing tools plus brief guidance suffice, stop new-product development and provide a configuration guide. Only prototype a smaller adapter if a shared, reproducible gap remains. Proposed thresholds—such as at least two participants sharing the gap and a 30% total-time reduction—are decision choices, not findings or industry standards.

## Changes prompted by the pilot

- Track population and geography fit separately from the existence of a complaint.
- Separate adjacent pains and same-project documentation from independent user evidence.
- Distinguish documented capability, attempted test and observed task success.
- Record exact source-date provenance; search snippets cannot establish an edited post's timeline.
- Preserve reuse and configuration guidance as successful research outcomes.

No private notes, original reference screenshots, usernames or bulk forum copies are included. This case is an illustration of a research decision, not a validation of profitability.
